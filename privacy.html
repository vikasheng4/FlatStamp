RentProof Privacy

RentProof is designed to keep rental inspection data on your device. It does not require an account or send inspection photos, notes, signatures, addresses, or meter readings to a RentProof server.

Local data

Your property records and photos are stored in your browser's local IndexedDB storage. Clearing site data or removing browser data may delete them. Use the in-app backup export for important records.

Sharing

When you print, save, export, or share a report, the destination you choose may process that data under its own privacy terms.

Hosting logs

The static hosting provider may process ordinary technical request information such as IP address and browser details when serving the site.

No analytics

This build contains no advertising SDK, analytics SDK, account system, or tracking pixel.
