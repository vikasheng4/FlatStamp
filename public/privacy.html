FlatStamp

Privacy, kept simple.

FlatStamp is local-first. It does not require an account and does not send inspection photos, notes, addresses, meter readings, or property records to a FlatStamp server.

Local data

Property records and photos are stored in your browser using IndexedDB. Clearing browser or site data can delete them. Export a backup after important inspections.

Sharing and PDF export

PDFs are generated on your device. When you save, export, copy, or share a record, the destination you choose may process that data under its own privacy terms.

Hosting logs

The static hosting provider may process ordinary technical request information, such as IP address and browser details, when serving app files.

No tracking

This MVP contains no advertising SDK, analytics SDK, tracking pixel, account system, payment processor, or external AI API.

Evidence disclaimer

FlatStamp helps organise condition records. It does not provide legal advice, independently prove authenticity, or guarantee the outcome of a deposit or tenancy dispute.
